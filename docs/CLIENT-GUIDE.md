# Ashnight — Client Guide

*For members who want a vetted ash (cleaning) specialist.*

---

## 1. What Ashnight is

Ashnight is members-only. You join a **room**, and inside that room you can browse
vetted specialists, chat with them, agree on the work, and pay. Your money sits in
**escrow** until the visit is done and you've raised no issue — so you're never paying
into thin air. All prices are in **GHS**.

---

## 2. Create your account

1. Go to **Sign up** (`/auth`) and register with your email and a password, or continue
   with Google.
2. Confirm your email address if prompted.
3. Complete your profile at **/profile**: photo, display name, city, a short bio, your
   languages, and your **likes / dislikes** (specialists use these to tailor the visit).
4. Optional but recommended: turn on **two-factor authentication** on the same page.
   You'll scan a QR code with an authenticator app and enter a 6-digit code at sign-in.

Every account is **manually vetted** before it can transact. You'll see your status on
your profile; once approved you're free to book.

---

## 3. Choose a room

Open **Rooms** (`/rooms`) to compare tiers. Each room has its own membership price and
its own privileges, for example:

- **Basic** — chat, standard response times, core services
- **Premium** — adds voice calls and file sharing, faster response SLA
- **Ultimate** — adds video calls, higher booking limits, priority support

Exact prices, limits and privileges are set by the platform admin and shown live on the
Rooms page. Pay the membership with Paystack (card, mobile money, or bank) and you're
placed in that room. Your room decides which specialists you can reach and which chat
features you get.

---

## 4. Find a specialist

**Specialists** (`/specialists`) lists everyone vetted and available in your room. Each
card shows their headline, city, hourly rate in GHS, response time, star rating, jobs
completed and verification badge. Open a profile to read their full bio, the services
they render, and their reviews.

Tap **Message** to start a thread.

---

## 5. Chat, calls, and agreeing the work

Chat (`/messages`) is realtime. Depending on your room you also get:

- **Voice call** and **Video call** buttons in the thread header
- **Attachments** — send a photo of the space so quotes are accurate

Discuss what you need, when, and for how long. Keep it in the chat: sharing phone
numbers, emails or social handles is blocked by default, which protects both sides and
keeps your escrow protection valid.

---

## 6. Request and pay for a visit

1. Tap the **payment icon** in the chat toolbar.
2. Pick the service, hours, date/time, any add-ons, and add notes.
3. Review the total — hourly rate × hours + add-ons — then pay through **Paystack**.
4. Your payment goes into **escrow**, not to the specialist.

You'll see a booking card in the thread tracking: *requested → accepted → paid →
completed*.

---

## 7. Escrow: how your money is protected

- Funds are **held** the moment you pay.
- After the visit the entry starts **clearing** for a hold window set by the platform.
- If you raise no issue, funds **release** to the specialist automatically.
- If something's wrong, tap **Raise an issue** on the booking before the window ends.
  The entry becomes **disputed**, payout stops, and an admin reviews the thread and
  decides: release or refund.

Never pay outside the platform — off-platform payments have no escrow, no dispute route,
and no refund.

---

## 8. Gifts and tips

Happy with the work? Send a **gift** from the chat. Gifts carry real cash value (set per
room by the admin) and are paid through Paystack into the same escrow flow, so the
specialist receives them after clearing. There's a maximum tip per transaction.

---

## 9. Rate and report

- **Star rating**: after a visit, tap the rating icon in the chat, give 1–5 stars, pick
  tags (punctual, thorough, respectful…) and add an optional note. Ratings drive the
  specialist's public score.
- **Report**: tap the report icon if someone breaks the rules — pick a reason, add
  details, and optionally block them. Admins see the report with the relevant thread
  excerpt.

---

## 10. Settings you control

On **/profile**: photo, bio, city, likes and dislikes, languages, notification-relevant
details, **theme** (light / dark / system) and **two-factor authentication**.

---

## 11. Good practice

- Be specific about the space, the surfaces and the time needed — accurate briefs get
  accurate quotes.
- Confirm the date and address in chat before paying.
- Rate every visit; it's what keeps the vetted pool high-quality.
- Raise issues inside the hold window, not after — that's when escrow can still act.

---

## 12. FAQ

**Why do I pay a membership?** It funds the manual vetting of every specialist and keeps
the rooms private.

**Do specialists pay to join?** No — specialists join free and pay only a commission on
completed work.

**Can I change rooms?** Yes, upgrade from **Rooms**; your access changes when the new
membership is active.

**Can I get my money back?** If a visit goes wrong, raise an issue before the escrow
window closes and an admin can refund you.

**Why can't I send my phone number?** Contact sharing is blocked to keep deals — and
your escrow protection — on the platform.
