# Ashnight — Specialist Guide

*For vetted professionals who deliver ash (cleaning) services and earn on Ashnight.*

---

## 1. What Ashnight is for you

Ashnight is a members-only marketplace where vetted clients pay to reach vetted
specialists. **You join free.** You don't pay a membership; the platform takes a
commission on completed work only. Every payment a client makes goes into **escrow**,
and it lands with you after the hold window closes with no issue raised. All amounts are
in **GHS**.

---

## 2. Apply and get vetted

1. Open **Apply** (`/apply`).
2. Submit: full name, email, phone, city, years of experience, your pitch, and the
   services you render (chosen from the admin's catalogue).
3. An admin reviews you manually — identity, background check, references.
4. You'll be **approved**, put **in review** with questions, or **rejected**.
5. On approval the admin assigns you to a **room** (Basic / Premium / Ultimate). Your
   room decides which clients can reach you and which chat features you get.

Applications can be paused by the admin; if the form is closed, check back later.

---

## 3. Set up your profile

On **/profile**:

- **Photo** — clear, professional; it's the first thing clients see
- **Headline** — one line on what you're best at
- **Bio** — experience, approach, what you bring
- **City**, **languages**, **years of experience**
- **Hourly rate (GHS)** — must sit inside your room's allowed visit-fee range
- **Services** — tick every service you actually deliver; only admin-created services
  are selectable
- **Availability** — toggle off when you're fully booked
- **Two-factor authentication** — strongly recommended, protects your earnings
- **Theme** — light / dark / system

Your rating, jobs completed and response time are shown publicly and are calculated from
real activity — you can't edit them.

---

## 4. Working in chat

Threads live at `/messages`. Depending on your room you get voice calls, video calls and
file attachments. Practical tips:

- **Reply fast.** Response time is public and each room has a response SLA.
- **Ask for photos** of the space before quoting.
- **Confirm scope, hours, date and address in writing** before the client pays.
- **Never move the deal off-platform.** Sharing phone numbers, emails or social handles
  is blocked, and off-platform work has no escrow protection, no dispute route, and can
  get you suspended.

---

## 5. Bookings

The client raises a booking request from the chat: service, hours, date/time, add-ons and
notes.

1. Review the request card — check the total and whether the timing works.
2. **Accept** it (or discuss changes in chat first).
3. The client pays through Paystack; the card moves to **paid** and the money is in
   escrow.
4. Deliver the visit, then mark it **completed**.

Rooms may cap how many bookings you can take per month — check your room's privileges.

---

## 6. Getting paid: escrow explained

```
client pays -> HELD -> CLEARING (hold window) -> RELEASED -> paid out to you
                 |
                 +-> DISPUTED (client raised an issue) -> admin releases or refunds
```

- Funds are visible to you as **held** immediately, but not yet yours.
- After completion the entry **clears** for the hold window the admin has configured.
- No issue raised → it **releases** automatically.
- Issue raised → an **admin reviews the thread** and decides. Good chat records are your
  best defence, which is another reason to keep everything on-platform.

**Your payout = booking total − platform commission.** The commission percentage is set
per room and shown on the Rooms page.

---

## 7. Gifts and tips

Clients can send gifts in chat that carry real cash value. Gifts run through the same
escrow flow (a tip commission applies and there's a per-tip ceiling), so they arrive with
your other cleared earnings.

---

## 8. Ratings and reputation

After a visit the client can leave 1–5 stars, tags and a note. Your average rating and
jobs-completed count sit on your public profile and directly affect how much work you
get. To protect it: arrive on time, be thorough, communicate, and finish what you quoted.

---

## 9. Safety

- **Report** any client who breaks the rules from the chat's report icon — pick a reason,
  add details, optionally block them. Admins see the report with the thread excerpt.
- Never accept work that falls outside the listed ash services.
- If a client pressures you to take payment off-platform, report it.

---

## 10. Rules that get you suspended

- Sharing or soliciting contact details to move a deal off-platform
- Requesting or accepting cash outside escrow
- No-shows, or delivering less than what was quoted
- Abusive language, harassment, or discrimination
- Misrepresenting your experience, identity or services

Suspension keeps your record but stops you messaging, booking and being booked.

---

## 11. FAQ

**Do I pay anything to join?** No. Free to join; commission on completed work only.

**Why is my payment still held?** The escrow hold window hasn't elapsed, or the client
raised an issue. Check the escrow status on the booking card.

**Who decides my room?** An admin, at vetting. You can ask to be reviewed for a higher
room as your rating and completed jobs grow.

**Why can't I set the rate I want?** Each room has a min/max visit fee so pricing stays
consistent for members.

**A client disputed unfairly — what now?** Reply in the thread with the facts; the admin
reviewing the dispute reads the full chat history.
